01 Original TCG · In development

WAR-TCG

A five-colour trading card game about a kingdom fighting on every front. I'm designing all of it: the rules, the colour pie, the balance math, the card frames, and the tools that keep it all in sync.

Card database & deck builder ↗

Card frames from my Affinity template. Each card is filled in by script from the card table. The art boxes are empty until illustration.

The design problems I set out to fix

  1. No mana screw

    A unit can be stationed in the Backline to make energy, or played to the Frontline to fight. Every card in hand is a resource, so you always have something to do.

  2. One number for everything

    There's no separate damage or life. Units, Leaders and Regions all have Might, so "−3 Might" can kill a unit, weaken a leader or chip at a Region.

  3. Every card defends

    When you're attacked you pitch cards from your hand for their Block value. Each card is a play and a shield, and that choice is the core of the game.

  4. Travel time on the board

    Three zones: Backline, Middle, Frontline. The Middle holds only one unit, which slows big board swings and lets the opponent see threats coming.

  5. Always answerable

    No hexproof or shroud, and no shuffling in the middle of a game. Searches look at the top cards and put the rest on the bottom, which keeps paper play fast.

Balance formula

Every unit is priced the same way. The spreadsheet checks each card against it.

value = might + block + production + text
value − cost ∈ [3, 5]

// each keyword            = 1
// coloured production     = 1, colourless a bit less
// Fee — X (a drawback)    = −X
// cheap cards sit near 3, expensive ones near 5

From the design log

Every rules change goes in the log with a date and a reason. A few entries:

  • May 2026

    Front / Back line. Units become resources when played to the Backline. This removes MTG-style mana screw without adding a separate land type.

  • Jun 2026

    Life replaced by Regions. Leaders can't be attacked any more. Your Regions hold your Might, so damage and removal use the same number.

  • Jun 2026

    Blocking subtracts from the attacker. It used to buff the defender. The change made multi-unit attacks work cleanly.

  • Aug 2026

    Defense and Layers split. Defense is the Might an attack needs to connect. Layers cap what one hit removes. Pierce beats Layers, not Defense.

02 Chess variant · Physical card game

CHARDSS

Cards, Chess & Chaos. It's chess with a shared deck of 40 cards, and every card bends one rule of chess.

The twist is in the economy: you earn points when your own pieces are captured and spend them on cards. A player who is losing material gets more options, so there's a comeback mechanic built into the resource system.

Costs run from 0 to 39. The odd one out is Armageddon. It costs 39, which is every point a player can ever earn, and it wins the game if your King is the only piece you have left.

  • 40 cards, 3-card hand, one card per turn
  • Custom font that turns text into chess-piece icons
  • Website with an animated board demo for every card

04 Digital game · Team project · 2024–25

Glory

A turn-based tactics game where your hand is your move set. Each character brings their own deck, their own weapons and their own resource curve to a grid battlefield.

I wrote the rules and designed the content. The rest of the team built a Python game server and a TypeScript client. Combat runs on a combat chain: attacker and defender trade offensive and defensive reactions, and the chain resolves last-in, first-out. Facing matters too, since hits from the back or side change what lands.

  • 10 characters, 75 cards, 10 weapons
  • Energy and Agility resources with per-character limits
  • Line of sight, range, push, pull and spin on a tile grid

05 Worldbuilding · Ongoing

The Wondering Emperor

An original fantasy-steampunk world in a Renaissance-like age. Hundreds of kingdoms live under a fragile alliance, and a conqueror's disappearance leaves a cycle of legacy, betrayal and revenge behind him.

A hard magic system

Mana is a finite, body-bound resource. Inner magic strengthens the body. Outer magic pushes mana into matter, and the result depends on the state of that matter:

Add manaDrain mana
Gasheatscools
Liquidexpandscontracts
Solidsoftens, can be shapedhardens

Each material has a maximum mana it can hold and a rate at which it leaks. Magic circles work as mana's programming language, which is how guns, transport and medicine run on it.

Power has a curve

Strength = Physical × Magical Prowess. Physical strength peaks around 30 and Prowess keeps growing, so a mage's power peaks around 55.

Shape only, not to scale.

Rules the world keeps

  • Armies are small. Even great kingdoms field under 1,000 soldiers, because a few mages decide wars.
  • Nobody can win alone. Even the strongest need allies or a plan.
  • Rivers never split. They only merge on the way to the sea.
  • Gifted children, born with abnormal mana and silver hair, are raised as royalty on an island commoners never see.
