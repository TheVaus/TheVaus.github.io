A One Piece cube format

I designed a custom cube format for One Piece TCG, with its own format rules and a pool of 540 cards.

What it brings to my designs

One Piece TCG is one of the main references for WAR-TCG. From it I kept:

  • Two uses for every card, through a block system where you pitch cards from your hand to defend.
  • Keyword blocks in the rules text, like On Play.

I left out its triggers, because players can't play around them.
