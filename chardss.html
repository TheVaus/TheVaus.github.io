Cards, Chess & Chaos. It's chess with a shared deck of 40 cards, and every card bends one rule of chess. You pay for cards with the pieces you lose.

The game

Both players draw from one shared deck. Each turn you may play one card, then make one move, then draw back to three cards. Every card bends one rule of chess: your Pawns capture sideways, your King swaps places with another piece, your opponent has to change their last move.

You pay for cards with Points, and you earn points when your own pieces are captured. The player who is behind on material has the most to spend.

What I've done

  • Rules. The rulebook, including the rules that keep rule-bending cards from breaking the game.
  • Cards. All 40 cards and their prices, from 0 to 39 points.
  • Card frames and font. The card fronts and back in Affinity, and a font that draws chess pieces in card text.
  • Website. Rules, every card with an animated board demo, and design notes, all built from the game's card data.
