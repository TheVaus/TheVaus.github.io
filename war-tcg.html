WAR-TCG

A five-colour trading card game about a kingdom fighting on every front. Any unit in your hand can become a resource, a threat or a shield, and you decide which.

The game

Your kingdom is at war on every front. Each player brings a Leader, which sets their identity, and three Regions, and picks one Region to defend after seeing the opponent's Leader. The ideas the game is built on:

  • Units are the resource. There are no land cards. Any unit can be stationed in your Backline to produce energy, or deployed to the Frontline to fight, so there's no mana screw.
  • Combat on one number. Units fight with Might alone. An attack hits if the attackers' Might reaches the target's.
  • Your hand is your defence. Pitch units from your hand to add their Block to whatever is being attacked.
  • Always answerable. No hexproof, no counters to track, no shuffling in the middle of a game.

What I've done

  • Rules. Five versions of the rules so far, with every decision logged with its reasoning.
  • Colour pie. Five colours, each with a play style, signature mechanics and mechanics it never gets, plus an identity for each of the ten colour pairs.
  • Balance. One pricing formula for every unit, a locked set size and a target cost curve.
  • Card frames. Built in Affinity and filled by a script from the card table, now with gold, silver and bronze frames for units, commands and items.
  • Tools. One card table feeds the card renders, the rules, the glossary and the website, which turns every change into a dated update.
