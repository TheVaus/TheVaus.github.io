One Piece TCG cube

A small project of mine: a cube format for One Piece TCG, where players draft their decks from a shared pool instead of bringing their own.

Format
Draft, 8 players (or 4 with double pick)
Card pool
540 cards (version 2, October 2026)
Design goal
Magic's cube archetypes, in One Piece's rules

The idea

A cube needs its own format rules and a curated card pool. I chose the cards and wrote the rules to adapt Magic deck archetypes to the One Piece ruleset.

Format rules

  1. One Leader for everyone. Every player uses Rainbow Luffy.
  2. Colours cost Life. You start with 6 Life if your deck has one colour, and 1 less for each colour beyond the first: 5 for two colours, 4 for three, and so on.
  3. The draft. Three packs of 16 cards. Pick 1 card and pass the rest, until the pack is gone. The first pack passes clockwise, the second counter-clockwise, the third clockwise again. That's 48 cards each, and you build a 30-card deck from them.
  4. Players. Made for 8 players. With 4, play double pick: the same rules, but you take 2 cards each time.

The Life rule is the cube's only colour restriction. You can play any colours you draft, but each extra colour costs you a Life.
