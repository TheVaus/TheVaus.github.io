One Piece TCG cube

A small project of mine: a cube format for One Piece TCG, where players build their decks from a shared pool instead of bringing their own.

Format
Custom cube for One Piece TCG
Card pool
540 cards
Design goal
Magic's cube archetypes, in One Piece's rules

The idea

A cube needs its own format rules and a curated card pool. I chose the cards and wrote the rules to adapt Magic deck archetypes to the One Piece ruleset.
