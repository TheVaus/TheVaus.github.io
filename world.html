The Wondering Emperor

An original world in a Renaissance-like age, with fantasy-steampunk capitals and a hard magic system. There are hundreds of kingdoms, and most of the land is held by a fragile alliance of seven. The disappearance of a conqueror leaves a cycle of legacy, betrayal and revenge behind him.

A hard magic system

Mana is a finite energy each person carries, like muscle strength. Overusing it leads to exhaustion or unconsciousness, and it comes back with rest, food, or time in places rich in mana. Magic splits in two:

  • Inner magic strengthens the body: speed, strength, durability and senses.
  • Outer magic pushes mana into matter. What happens depends on the state of that matter.
MatterAdding manaDraining mana
GasHeats itCools it
LiquidExpands itContracts it
SolidSoftens it so it can be shapedHardens it

Every material has two numbers: how much mana it can hold and how fast it leaks. A plant that conducts mana works as cable, and magic circles act as mana's programming language. That's how this world gets guns, transport, communication and medicine.

Power has a curve

A mage's power is Physical Strength × Magical Prowess. Physical strength peaks around 30 and then falls. Prowess keeps growing all their life. Together they put a mage's peak around 55.

Rare Gifted children are born with abnormal mana, silver hair and long lives. They're raised as royalty on an island that commoners never see, and they become generals, guards and advisors.

Rules the world keeps

  • Armies are small. Even great kingdoms field fewer than 1,000 soldiers, because a few strong mages decide wars.
  • Nobody wins alone. Even the strongest characters need allies or a plan.
  • Rivers never split. They only merge on the way to the sea, and port cities sit where big rivers reach the sea.
  • The land is huge. Ancient forests have trees the size of giant sequoias, so people feel small.

How it's built

The world lives in an Obsidian vault of about 120 linked notes. Characters, cities, kingdoms, events and races each use a template with structured properties, so tables of characters and events build themselves. There's a family-tree canvas and a full timeline split into sagas.
